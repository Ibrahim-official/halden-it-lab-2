# Halden Distribution Ltd. — Guest Wi-Fi acceptable-use notice

**Display this before a voucher is issued.** One copy at reception, one at the visitor sign-in desk.

---

## Welcome to Halden

Guest Wi-Fi is provided as a courtesy for visitors, contractors and interviewees. It gives you
**access to the internet, and nothing else**. Please read this short notice before you connect; by
using the guest network you accept it.

## What you may do

- Use the internet — email, web browsing, video calls, mobile apps.

## What you may not do

- **Do not attempt to access Halden's systems, servers, printers or other visitors' devices.** The
  network is deliberately separated and such attempts are recorded.
- **Do not use the network for anything unlawful**, offensive, or that could harm Halden or anyone
  else — including downloading or sharing illegal material.
- **Do not run scanning, hacking or intrusion tools** from the guest network.
- **Do not use the guest network for large downloads or streaming** that would slow the connection for
  other visitors.
- **Do not connect a Halden device** to the guest network; staff devices use the corporate network.

## What we record

- The time a voucher was issued and how long it was used.
- Connection and disconnection times.
- We do **not** ask for personal information to issue a voucher beyond your name and who you are
  visiting, and we do not monitor the content of your traffic.

## What happens if this notice is broken

Access is withdrawn immediately. If the activity appears to be an attempt to reach our systems,
Halden will record it and may report it. That sounds heavy for a Wi-Fi notice, but it is the same
position any business has to take.

## Getting connected

1. Ask at reception for a guest voucher.
2. Connect to the Wi-Fi network named **Halden-Guest**.
3. Enter the voucher code when the welcome page appears.
4. The voucher is valid for a single device and a limited time. Ask reception if you need another.

**Having trouble?** Ask reception to contact IT. Please do not ask a passing member of staff to
"just let you on the normal Wi-Fi" — that network is for company devices only.

---

> **Note for reception staff:** issue one voucher per device, per visitor. Do not share a voucher
> between devices or visitors, and do not give out the corporate Wi-Fi details — corporate devices
> connect with a certificate, not a password. If a visitor cannot connect, raise a ticket rather than
> improvising (`../docs/runbooks/add-a-device-to-a-zone.md` explains how guest devices are placed).
