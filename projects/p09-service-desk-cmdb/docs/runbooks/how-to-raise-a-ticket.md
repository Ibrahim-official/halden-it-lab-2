# How to raise a ticket at Halden (user guide)

**For:** all Halden Distribution Ltd. staff · **Owner:** IT Service Desk · **Review:** annually

> Halden Distribution Ltd. is the fictional company used in this home-lab portfolio. This guide is
> written as it would be for real staff so the service desk can be demonstrated end to end.

## The short version

One place, one ticket, one reference number. If it is not raised as a ticket, IT cannot track it,
prioritise it, or show the business how much work is being done or what keeps breaking.

## Where to raise it

| Channel | Use it for | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-service portal** (preferred) | Anything that is not stopping work: access, software, hardware, new starter, restore a file | You choose the service, answer a short form, and get a ticket number immediately |
| **Email to the Service Desk** | When you are not near a browser | The email is turned into a ticket automatically (email collector) |
| **Phone** | Only when work is stopped (see priorities) | The desk raises the ticket for you |

Every ticket gets a **reference number** — quote it in any follow-up so we can find it in seconds.

## What to include (this is the whole trick)

A good ticket is answered faster. Please include:

1. **What you were doing** and **what happened** (the exact message helps).
2. **When it started** and **whether it is still happening**.
3. **Who is affected** — just you, your team, or everyone.
4. **The device name** (the sticker on the laptop) or the **app/website** you were using.
5. **A screenshot** of the error if you can.

## How quickly we respond

Targets are in **business hours** (Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00). "Time to own" is when someone takes the
ticket; "time to resolve" is when it is fixed.

| Priority | Looks like | Someone on it within | Resolved within |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Whole site down, ransomware, payroll-day failure | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | A department cannot work, VPN down for everyone remote | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 Medium | You cannot work, but there is a workaround | 4 hours | 2 business days |
| P4 Low | A request or something cosmetic | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Before you raise a ticket (saves you time)

Some things you can do yourself, or a knowledge-base article solves in two minutes:

- **A file was deleted or changed:** use **Previous Versions** on the shared drive (see the P1 guide).
- **A drive letter is missing:** sign out and back in once.
- **Your account is locked:** wait 15 minutes, or call the desk — lockouts are resolved with an
  identity check.
- **A phishing email arrived:** do not click; forward it to the security address and raise a
  "Report phishing" ticket.

## What happens after you raise it

1. The desk reads the ticket, sets the priority and owns it.
2. If it needs someone else, it is escalated to Systems (L2) or a vendor (L3) with your ticket
   number attached, so you never have to explain it twice.
3. You get told when it is resolved; if the fix is not right, reply on the same ticket and it is
   reopened.

## Please do not

- Send the same issue by email, Teams and WhatsApp "just in case" — it creates duplicate work.
- Put passwords in a ticket. The desk will never ask for your password; a password reset is done
  with an identity check instead.
- Raise a "down" ticket as a low-priority request. If work is stopped, say so; we would rather set
  the priority correctly at the start.

## Who to contact

| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| Anything on this page | IT Service Desk (portal or email) |
| Work is stopped right now | IT Service Desk by phone, say "work is stopped" |
| You think you clicked a phishing link | Security — raise a "Report phishing" ticket immediately |
